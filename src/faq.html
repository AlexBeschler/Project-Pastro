Frequently Asked Questions

How does Pastro know my name?
It gets this information from your Google account. When you choose to sign in with the account, Google passes only a few pieces of information to Pastro such as your name and the profile picture associated to your account. In order to deliver a personalized experience we display this info throughout the app. This information isn't stored on our servers - we get it straight from Google. If you change your name or profile picture, Pastro will reflect the new information accordingly.

What personal information does Pastro store?
Your cookbook data and the app settings. That's it! In addition, no third parties read any part of your cookbook data. As a reminder:
  • Please read our Privacy Policy for further information regarding how your data is stored
  • We use Google Analytics for internal reporting purposes. Your details of app usage might be collected. For further information, please read the Google Analytics FAQs

Can I use multiple Google accounts with Pastro?
No. All your cookbook data and app settings are store per Google account. Let's say you control two Google accounts. You choose to sign up and pay with your primary Google account. One day you sign into Pastro with your secondary account - Pastro won't recognize you as paying customer! This is because your personal Stripe portal, cookbook, and account settings are all associated with a particular Google account email address. A second Google account is considered a different "identity".

Can I merge two or more Google accounts into one with Pastro?
At this time, no.

Do I need to sign into Pastro with a password?
No. When you first sign into Pastro, it will redirect you to a Google account log-in page. Pastro doesn't read any of this information; rather, behind the scenes Pastro is asking Google for permission to use the account, and that permission is granted when you successfully sign into Google. Once Google verifies it's you, then Pastro verifies the permission is granted by Google (and not by someone malicious!) and loads all your personalized data to you. Remember - as long as your Google account is safe, so is Pastro; no extra passwords required!

How are payments managed?
By a company called Stripe. When signing up for Pastro for the first time, you have to enter valid payment information into the Stripe portal. When you do, the page will re-direct you to the app, ready for you to use it.

How do I change or cancel my subscription?
Simply click the settings menu and scroll to the very bottom. Click the button that says "Stripe Portal" and you can manage all payment information from there.

How do I request a refund?
While we do not issue refunds for past purchases, you may cancel a subscription early. If you choose to do so, the remainder of the month will be credited to you.

Something looks wrong about my billing. How can I get in touch with someone?
We want to help your resolve this issue as soon as possible. Please contact us here.

Does Pastro handle any payment information?
No. The only payment-related information Pastro stores are receipts, charge history (records related to the monthly subscription charges), and any relevant sales tax information. This info can be displayed in your personalized Stripe billing portal. All other forms of payment are solely managed by Stripe.

Are there iOS and Android apps?
Not at this time. Pastro is a mobile-first, highly optimized website that functions like a regular app. You can even "install" it, and it will live in your app drawer or Start menu (depending on what platform you are on). If you click the icon it will open your web browser and you can use it like normal.

Do you have plans to develop iOS and Android apps?
It's not currently on our company roadmap. The process is expensive and very time consuming. Remember - we're a development team of one!
