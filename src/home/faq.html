Billing

Payments are managed by a company called Stripe. When signing up for Pantry for the first time, you have to enter valid payment information into the Stripe portal. When you do, the page will re-direct you to the app, ready for you to use it.

Simply click the settings menu located in the app and scroll to the very bottom. Click the button that says "Stripe Portal" and you can manage all payment information from there.

While we do not issue refunds for past purchases, you may cancel a subscription early. If you choose to do so, the remainder of the month will be credited to you.

Account & Privacy

  • Please read our Privacy Policy for further information regarding how your data is stored
  • We use Google Analytics for internal reporting purposes. Your details of app usage might be collected. For further information, please read the Google Analytics Privacy Disclosures

Your cookbook data, app settings, and any Stripe recipes/tax information are stored across various services run by Firebase, a Google-owned company. Our database is located somewhere in central US, and the website resources are spread across various web servers around the world. OCR data is initially processed with AI models operated by Google, and no photo information is stored with them.

It's fairly safe. While there is no guarantee that your data is 100% safe, Pantry uses industry standards for handling and storing your data. We use HTTPS (meaning encrypted) connections to most external resources, and special controls for accessing data. Any publicly accessible resources (such as recipes you manually shared with others) are obfuscated using IDs called UUIDs. Even our admin tools have special controls in place that prevent unauthorized access.
